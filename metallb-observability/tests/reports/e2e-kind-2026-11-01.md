## What the e2e caught (fixes now in the suite)

1. **S7 repair bug:** reverting the blackout with a strategic-merge patch *merges*
   `nodeSelector` maps — the blackout key survived and the speakers never came back.
   Fixed with a json `replace` patch.
2. **S8 expectation bug:** `MetalLBK8sClientErrors` firing right after S4/S7 is the alert
   *working* — the pod churn produces real `k8s_client_update_errors_total` increments
   (verified in the TSDB). The check now expects it to resolve, not to have stayed quiet.
3. **Laptop VM freezes defeat the missed-iterations stall guard** (the counter can't
   increment while the clock is frozen): alerts sat `pending` for 5+ minutes with their
   expr continuously true. `lib.sh` now also measures the gap in Prometheus's own
   self-scrape (`sample_gap_pct`) and extends waits when ≥25% of samples are missing.
   A 100%-clean single run needs the host awake throughout (~30 min): `./e2e/scenarios.sh`.
