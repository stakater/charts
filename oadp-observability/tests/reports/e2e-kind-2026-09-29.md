## Follow-up: 6b

**6b failed in this run because of the test, not the rule.** After the Service was deleted,
Prometheus wrote no staleness marker, so the last `up` sample stayed visible for the 5-minute
lookback and `absent()` only turned true then. The alert went `firing` 2 seconds after the
test's 240-second timeout. The timeout now covers the documented worst case (5m lookback +
`for` + margin = 480s). That latency is recorded in `docs/design.md` and `docs/runbook.md`.

**Re-run of 6b alone** ([`e2e-kind-2026-09-29-only-6b.md`](e2e-kind-2026-09-29-only-6b.md)):
